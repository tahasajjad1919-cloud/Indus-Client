Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the Indus Client launcher, the in-game Indus Client mod and this website use, why, and what choices you have. We collect as little as we can. We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

1. Information that stays on your computer

  • Your Minecraft account. When you sign in with Microsoft, the sign-in happens on Microsoft's own page. The launcher keeps your account name, UUID and the sign-in tokens Microsoft gives it, stored encrypted on your computer, so you don't have to sign in every time. We never see your Microsoft password.
  • Settings, profiles and logs. Your launcher settings, game profiles, mod settings, HUD layout and game logs are saved on your computer only.

2. Information sent to the Indus service

While you play with Indus Client, the game talks to the Indus service so that cosmetics and the Indus logo next to names work for everyone. It sends:

  • your Minecraft UUID and username,
  • which cosmetics you are wearing,
  • the UUIDs of players in the same server as you, so it can tell which of them also use Indus Client.

When you use these launcher features, it also sends:

  • Signing in to the Indus service: for Microsoft accounts, the launcher proves you own the account through Mojang's own session server (the same check Minecraft servers use). Your Microsoft tokens never reach us. Offline accounts get a random secret stored on your computer, so nobody else can take your offline name.
  • Friends (Microsoft accounts only): your friend list and requests, and a simple status (“In the launcher” or “Playing Minecraft”) that only your friends see.
  • Skin changer (offline accounts): the skin picture you choose, so other Indus players can see it.
  • Support tickets: the name, contact (Discord or email), topic and messages you write in a ticket, so the Indus team can answer you. If you open a ticket while signed in, it is linked to your account. We keep the newest 500 tickets; you can ask us to delete yours at any time.
  • Discord link: your Discord user ID, display name and whether you are in the Indus Discord server, so we can unlock Discord rewards. We never get your Discord password or messages.

The service also sees your IP address, like any website, which we use only to stop abuse (rate limiting). It does not receive your chat, your inventory, the servers you play on, or your Microsoft tokens. To show the Indus emblem next to chat messages, Indus Client checks chat lines on your own computer; they are never sent to us.

We keep who is online for a few minutes only. We keep the list of cosmetics you own, your friends, your custom skin, your Discord link and the username linked to your UUID while you use Indus Client, so they keep working. You can remove your custom skin at any time from the launcher.

3. Purchases

Cosmetics are bought through the official Indus Client Discord. To unlock your purchase we need your Minecraft username or UUID. Payments are handled by the payment method you use there; we don't receive or store your full card details. Discord's own privacy policy applies to what you do on Discord.

4. Other services Indus Client connects to

To work, Indus Client connects to services run by others. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Microsoft and Mojang: signing in, checking that you own Minecraft, and downloading the game, skins and capes.
  • Fabric, Forge and Modrinth: downloading mod loaders and performance mods.
  • OptiFine (optifine.net): the 1.8.9 profile downloads OptiFine from its official site.
  • Discord: only if you choose to link your Discord account.
  • GitHub: checking for and downloading Indus Client updates.
  • Minecraft servers you join, which see the same things they see from any Minecraft player.
  • Google Fonts: this website loads its fonts from Google.

5. This website

This website doesn't use tracking cookies or analytics. It remembers your ticket codes and the skin name you typed in your own browser (local storage) so you can come back to them; the skin preview loads pictures from mc-heads.net. The server that hosts it may keep standard access logs (such as IP address and pages visited) for security.

6. Children

Indus Client is meant for Minecraft players of all ages, but we don't knowingly collect more than the Minecraft UUID and username described above. If you are a parent and think your child has bought something or shared information with us, contact us and we'll help.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off Show Other Indus Players and Indus Logo By Names in Cosmetics settings.
  • You can sign out and remove your account from the launcher at any time, which deletes its stored tokens from your computer.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information the Indus service keeps about your UUID (deleting it also removes cosmetics you bought). Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR; contact us to use them.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the Indus service and keep stored data to a minimum. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee that information will never be accessed without permission.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page. For important changes we'll also post a notice on our Discord.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Message us on the official Indus Client Discord.
