Support

Is Indus Client free?

Yes. Indus Client is free to download and use.

Which versions are supported?

The launcher plays every release from 26.3 down to 1.8.9. The Indus menus and built-in mods are in 26.3, 1.21.9 – 1.21.11 and 1.8.9 (Forge).

How do I get cosmetics?

Earn coins by playing, daily rewards, quests and the spin wheel, then buy cosmetics in the launcher's Wardrobe or the in-game Store. You can also buy them with money on the Indus Discord, or open a mystery box.

What do I get with VIP?

A gold + next to your Indus emblem, VIP-only cosmetics, double coins, 20% off the coin shop, your own menu wallpaper, Indus Music, a custom kill sound and a soundboard. Get it with coins in the launcher or on the Discord.

How do I use emotes?

Press G in game and pick one. Wave, Clap, Salute and Facepalm are free; buy the others with coins in the launcher's Wardrobe (Emotes tab). Every Indus player sees your emotes.

How do I add my own kill sound?

VIPs: put a kill.wav file in the indus-sounds folder of your game folder, and soundboard clips (.wav) in indus-sounds/soundboard. Press N in game to open the soundboard.

Can cracked (offline) accounts use friends and cosmetics?

Yes. Friends, chat, coins and cosmetics work with Microsoft and cracked accounts.

Do I need to own Minecraft?

To play on online servers like Hypixel you need a Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition. Offline accounts work in singleplayer and offline-mode servers.

Windows or my browser warns about the download. Is it safe?

Yes. Indus Client isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen and some browsers show a notice for new apps. In the browser choose Keep, and in Windows click More info → Run anyway. The warning goes away as more players download it.
